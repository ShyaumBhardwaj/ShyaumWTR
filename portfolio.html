Professional Reflection

These three co-op terms provided invaluable insights into professional software development and systems engineering. The progression from web development to enterprise mainframe operations revealed the breadth of career possibilities in computer science.

Moving between roles taught me the importance of clear communication, thorough documentation, and understanding how individual contributions fit into larger organizational systems. Working on mission-critical infrastructure instilled a deep appreciation for reliability, security, and the human impact of technology decisions.

Most importantly, I learned that great engineering is not just about writing code—it's about understanding business needs, supporting teammates, and creating solutions that make meaningful differences.
